01 / THE ATHLETE

A career built one fight at a time.

Afonso Correia is a Portuguese kickboxing athlete representing Academia Ricardo Fernandes, with experience in federation competitions, national fight nights and international events.

He competes in K1 and Low Kick. In November 2026, he finished fifth in the ISKA Amateur World Championships K1 -60 kg division, which featured 24 entrants. His competitive journey reflects clear sporting goals and a commitment to continuous improvement.

From the 2023 national Kick Light title in Matosinhos, recorded in the athlete’s federation booklet, to the 2025 Iberian Open title in Viana do Castelo reported by the team, his journey also includes a 2024 Portuguese Cup victory and a Jovem Promessa do Futuro runner-up result, both team-reported. Sources and verification status are shown beside each result.

Independent sources and official results are linked on this page. Information from the athlete’s team is identified when a public fight record could not be verified.
